Functional Fitness Planner

Functional Fitness Planner helps beginners build simple fitness habits with clear workouts for strength, cardio, and mobility. Users can choose a workout based on their time, difficulty level, and fitness goal.

Strength

Build muscle, increase strength, and improve power. These workouts focus on helping you become stronger while supporting muscle growth.

Cardio

Improve your endurance, heart health, and energy levels. Cardio workouts help you move longer with less fatigue in everyday life and exercise.

Mobility: Increase flexibility, improve movement, and keep your body functioning at its best. These sessions combine mobility work with light strength and cardio to help you move better and reduce stiffness.
